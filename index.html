📖 Which Way to Go! - Official Rules

Goal: Travel from START to STOP across Dhaka with maximum points and leftover currency.

🚦 Traffic System

In Round 1, all roads are Medium traffic. From Round 2 onward, a 6-sided dice roll sets which road colors (Red, Green, Blue) are Heavy, Medium, and Light.

🚗 Vehicles & Speed Chart

  • 🏃 Walk/Run: Free (0 cost). Always earns 3 pts.
  • 🏍️ Bike: Cost 3. Speed: 3 (Heavy), 6 (Med), 9 (Light).
  • 🚗 Car: Cost 6. Speed: 9 (Heavy), 12 (Med), 18 (Light).
  • 🚌 Bus: Cost 3. Speed: 6 (Heavy), 9 (Med), 12 (Light). Only boards/alights at Bus Stations.
  • 🚂 Train: Cost 9. Fixed 15 pts speed! Runs exclusively on Railroad tracks and ignores traffic completely.

🎴 Ability Cards

Landing on an 'A' node lets you draw 2 cards: keep 1, discard 1. Cards give speed boosts, extra money, roadblock controls, or steal tokens and points from opponents.

🏁 Scoring & Endgame

  • Points milestones: Crossing 33, 66, and 100 pts awards +9 currency (3 coins).
  • Fastest Player Bonus: First to reach STOP gets +15 points.
  • Money Bonus: 1 leftover currency = +1 point.
  • Distance Penalty: Remaining players lose 6 points per stop from STOP.
