🚦 How Which Way to Go! Works:

1 Opening Draft & Cards Draft 1 of 2 secret Ability Cards at start, and draw more on 🎴A nodes (1 draw per node) or visit ✚ Hospitals (+3 Currency).
2 Pick the Right Vehicle 🏃 Walk (Free: 3 pts) • 🏍️ Bike (2 cost: 8/8/10 pts) • 🚌 Bus (3 cost: 6/10/13 pts) • 🚗 Car (6 cost: 6/12/19 pts) • 🚂 Train (6 cost: 16 pts).
3 Beat Dhaka Traffic Bikes weave through Heavy traffic (8 pts), Buses excel at stations, Cars dominate Light roads (19 pts), and Trains ignore traffic!
4 Fair Final Round When a player hits STOP (+12 pts 1st / +6 pts 2nd), the round finishes! Leftover money = +1 pt/unit; un-finished lose 6 pts/stop.

📖 Which Way to Go! - Official Rules

Goal: Navigate from START to STOP across Dhaka with the highest total score (combining travel points, finisher bonus, and leftover currency).

🎁 Setup & Opening Ability Card Draft

Every player starts at START with 12 Currency (4 coins) and draws 2 random Ability Cards, choosing 1 to keep secretly before Round 1 begins.

🚦 Traffic & Movement Rules

In Round 1, all roads have Medium traffic. From Round 2+, a 6-sided dice roll assigns Heavy, Medium, and Light traffic to Red, Green, and Blue roads. You may move forward to the next depth level or laterally across the current level (no backward moves or immediate ping-ponging).

🚗 Vehicles, Costs & Points (Heavy / Medium / Light)

  • 🏃 Walk / Run (Cost 0): Always earns 3 pts on any road.
  • 🏍️ Bike (Cost 2): Earns 8 (Heavy) • 8 (Med) • 10 (Light) pts. Weaves through Heavy traffic! Once boarded at a Bike Station (🏍️), keep riding on consecutive road turns.
  • 🚌 Public Bus (Cost 3): Earns 6 (Heavy) • 10 (Med) • 13 (Light) pts. Boards only at nodes with a Bus Station (🚌).
  • 🚗 Car / Taxi (Cost 6): Earns 6 (Heavy) • 12 (Med) • 19 (Light) pts. Fastest on Light highways, stuck in Heavy gridlock! Board at a Car Station (🚗) or play "Call a Uber", and keep riding on consecutive road turns.
  • 🚂 Express Train (Cost 6): Earns Fixed 16 pts on curved Railroad tracks from Train Stations (🚂). Completely immune to road traffic and roadblocks!

🎴 Ability Cards & ✚ Hospital Stations

Landing on an 'A' node for the first time lets you draw 2 cards (keep 1, discard 1). Visiting a ✚ Hospital station for the first time grants +3 Currency transit relief.

🏁 Milestones, Fair Final Round & Scoring

  • Score Milestones: Crossing 35, 70, and 105 pts awards +6 Currency (2 coins).
  • Equal-Turn Final Round: When the first player reaches STOP, the current round finishes so all players get equal turns!
  • Finisher Bonuses: 1st to reach STOP gets +12 pts; 2nd to reach STOP gets +6 pts.
  • Leftover Currency Bonus: 1 remaining Currency = +1 point.
  • Distance Penalty: Players who have not reached STOP lose 5 points per node distance from STOP.

🚀 Publish Online via GitHub Pages

Yes! You can publish this entire game completely free on GitHub Pages. Because Which Way to Go! uses 100% client-side HTML5 Canvas, Web Audio API, and serverless WebSocket rooms, it requires zero backend hosting cost!

Step 1: Push Code to your GitHub Repository

Commit all the files and push them to your repository (umahbub/WhichWay2Go):

git add .
git commit -m "Launch Which Way to Go Online Multiplayer"
git push origin main
Step 2: Enable GitHub Pages in 3 Clicks
  1. Go to your repository on GitHub: github.com/umahbub/WhichWay2Go
  2. Click on Settings ➔ Pages (in the left sidebar).
  3. Under Build and deployment ➔ Source, choose Deploy from a branch.
  4. Select Branch: main, Folder: / (root), and click Save.
Step 3: Share Your Live Game Link!

Within 1–2 minutes, GitHub will build your site and publish it live at:

https://umahbub.github.io/WhichWay2Go/

Anyone with this link can visit on Desktop or Mobile, create a room, share the Game ID, and play together over the internet!
